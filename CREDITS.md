**Taskbar Retrogames Heroes** is free software. The *program itself* is distributed at no cost under the MIT License.

## Sprites

Character sprites bundled with the app were collected from **public / open community sources** used by the retro-gaming and fan community — primarily sheet archives such as [The Spriters Resource](https://www.spriters-resource.com/) and similar open repositories.

These assets belong to their respective game publishers and original artists. This project:

- does **not** claim ownership of any game character or artwork
- uses sprites only for a **non-commercial, freeware desktop toy**
- redistributes the *application* freely; sprite rights remain with their owners

If you are a rights holder and want an asset removed, open an issue or contact us via [Telegram](https://t.me/gamebase54).
